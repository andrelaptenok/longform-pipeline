# The hours nobody pays for

Many young people say that volunteering is a good thing. But not many young
people do it. I want to write about this in this article.

First I want to write about what young people get from volunteering. A student
who works in an animal shelter for two months learns many good things. She
learns to come on time. She learns to work with people she does not know. She
learns to finish her work even when nobody says thank you. These things are good
for her life and for her future work. It is also good to feel that other people
need you. This is a very good feeling and young people do not have it very
often.

Second I want to write about why not many young people volunteer. One reason is
that they do not have time, because school is very hard in the matura year.
Another reason is that they do not know where to go, because organisations do
not tell them. Another reason is that they are afraid to go alone to a place
where they do not know anybody.

In my opinion these problems are not big problems. A noticeboard in school is
enough. A teacher can make a telephone call. A friend can come with you on the
first day. Then it is easy to help other people.
