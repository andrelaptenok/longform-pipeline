Dear Sir or Madam,

Should a refund be impossible, I would accept a place on a future course free of
charge. The accommodation was comfortable and the residence staff could not have
been more helpful. I am writing about the intensive English course I attended in
Kraków between 3 and 21 July.

There were nineteen of us in the room. Your advertisement promised groups of no
more than eight participants. The language laboratory shown in your photographs
proved to be a cupboard holding six headsets, four of which did not work. On
eleven of the fifteen days the class was led by a trainee who told us openly
that he was standing in for somebody else. The advertisement also promised
lessons taught by experienced teachers and four hours of tuition every morning.

I would ask you to refund the difference between the course I paid for and the
course I received, which I estimate at roughly half the total. My objection
concerns the teaching alone, which is what the fee was charged for. On two
mornings the session was cancelled altogether and replaced by a film in the
common room. You should confirm in writing that the group size and the teaching
staff will be as advertised. The course differed considerably from the course
described on your website.

I look forward to your reply.

Yours faithfully,

A. Nowak
