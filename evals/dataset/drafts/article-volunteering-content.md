Hi Marta,

How are things? Sorry I did not write earlier, the week was mad.

So you asked me about the volunteering thing and honestly I think you should
just do it. I have been at the animal shelter since March and it has changed
more than I expected. You learn to turn up on time, you work next to people you
would never have chosen, and you finish jobs nobody thanks you for. That sounds
boring written down, but it does something to you, and it says more about a
person than any certificate from a weekend workshop. Also, and this is the part
nobody mentions, it is nice to be needed. At our age that does not happen very
often.

The funny thing is that almost nobody from our year does anything like it, even
though they all say it is a great idea. I think it is three things. Matura year
eats every evening and weekend. Nobody knows where to go, because the
organisations only advertise to people who already volunteer. And walking into a
building full of strangers on your own is terrifying, whatever everybody
pretends.

Anyway, come with me on Saturday and you will see. That is honestly the whole
distance between wanting to help and helping.

Write back!

Love,
Ola
