Many schools now give tablets to their pupils. The pupils do not carry books any
more. Some people think this is good and some people think this is bad. I want
to write about learning and about money.

First, I want to write about learning. A tablet is good for learning because it
has a lot of books inside it. A pupil can find a difficult word very fast. A
teacher can send back an essay very fast too. But a tablet is also bad for
learning. A pupil can open a chat and talk to friends. Then the pupil does not
read the book. A paper book is not bad in this way, because you cannot open a
chat in a paper book.

Second, I want to write about money. A paper book is not expensive and a school
can use it for many years. A tablet is expensive. Tablets break when pupils drop
them, and then the family must pay for this. Some families do not have money for
this. Schools also need good internet and many schools do not have good
internet.

In conclusion, I think tablets are good but they are also bad. I think schools
should have tablets and books together. If a school has money and good rules,
tablets are good for pupils. If a school buys tablets only because it is modern,
this is bad for pupils.
