Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing about the English course in Kraków. I was on this course from 3 to
21 July. The course was not the same as on your website. I want to tell you
about this problem.

On your website you wrote that the groups were small and there were only eight
people. But in my group there were nineteen people. This was a big problem,
because the room was small and it was very difficult to speak. You also wrote
that the teachers were very good and had a lot of experience. But our teacher
was a young man who was still a student. He said to us that the other teacher
was ill. He was our teacher for eleven days of the fifteen days. Two times in
the morning there was no lesson and we watched a film. In the photos on your
website there is a language laboratory, but in reality it was only a small room
with six headsets, and four headsets did not work.

The rooms in the residence were good and the people there were very nice. My
problem is only the lessons, because I paid money for the lessons.

I want you to give me back half of the money. If this is not possible, I want to
go to another course for free. But first you must tell me in writing that the
group will be small and the teacher will be a real teacher.

I look forward to your reply.

Yours faithfully,

A. Nowak
