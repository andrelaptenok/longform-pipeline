# My story with Spanish

Languages have always been important, and today they are more important than
ever. Everybody knows somebody who has been learning Spanish for four years and
still cannot order a coffee. I was that person, and I would like to share my
story with the readers of this website.

I first became interested in Spanish because of a holiday. My aunt lives near
Valencia, and when I was fifteen we spent two weeks there in a small flat with a
balcony over a courtyard where somebody played the guitar every evening. I
remember the food, the heat, the noise of the market in the morning, and the
feeling of understanding nothing at all. That fortnight stayed with me for
years, and it is the reason I began at all.

There are of course many methods, and all of them work to some extent: apps,
films, workbooks, and talking to people online. Everyone has to find what suits
them best.

Motivation is the other half of the problem. It fades, as everybody knows, and
then it comes back, and the important thing is simply not to give up when it
does.

Learning a language outside school is possible for anybody who really wants it.
If my story convinces one reader to start, this article will have been worth
writing.
