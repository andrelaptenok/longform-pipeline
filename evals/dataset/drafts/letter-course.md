Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing about the intensive English course I attended in Kraków between 3
and 21 July, which differed considerably from the course described on your
website.

Your advertisement promised groups of no more than eight participants, lessons
taught by experienced teachers, and four hours of tuition every morning. In
practice there were nineteen of us in the room, and on eleven of the fifteen
days the class was led by a trainee who told us openly that he was standing in
for somebody else. On two mornings the session was cancelled altogether and
replaced by a film in the common room. The language laboratory shown in your
photographs proved to be a cupboard holding six headsets, four of which did not
work.

I should add that the accommodation was comfortable and the residence staff
could not have been more helpful. My objection concerns the teaching alone,
which is what the fee was charged for.

I would therefore ask you to refund the difference between the course I paid for
and the course I received, which I estimate at roughly half the total. Should a
refund be impossible, I would accept a place on a future course free of charge,
provided you confirm in writing that the group size and the teaching staff will
be as advertised.

I look forward to your reply.

Yours faithfully,

A. Nowak
