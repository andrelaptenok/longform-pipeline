Many schools now give tablets to their pupils instead of printed books, and the
change has clear advantages and disadvantages.

For learning, a tablet is useful. It holds an entire library, a difficult word
can be looked up in seconds, and a teacher can return corrected work the same
evening. The problem is distraction: a pupil who opens a group chat during a
reading task has stopped reading.

The costs are harder to defend. A textbook is bought once and used for years,
while a tablet breaks, needs replacing and requires a network the school may not
have.

In my opinion tablets should be added to schools, not used to replace books
entirely.
